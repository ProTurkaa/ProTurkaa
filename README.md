## 🏗️ Featured Projects

### [🚀 OmniPOD — Print-on-Demand Production Engine](https://github.com/ProTurkaa/Pod_Platform)
A vertically integrated, dual-sided POD infrastructure that bridges manufacturers and digital sellers. Providers manage blueprints, pricing, and logistics while sellers personalize catalog products through a professional **Fabric.js v7** design studio, set profit margins, and publish listings across global marketplaces (Etsy, Amazon, Shopify) — all from a single dashboard.

> `Next.js 15` · `Supabase` · `Clerk` · `Fabric.js` · `Framer Motion`

---

### [💊 MedicationAlert — Smart Medication Companion](https://github.com/ProTurkaa/MedicationAlert-)
A personal medication tracking application that helps users stay on top of their prescription schedules. Users can log their medications, receive intelligent reminders based on their usage habits, and monitor their personal stock levels in real-time. When a medication reaches critical stock, the app automatically identifies and routes the user to the nearest pharmacy that carries it.

> **Scalability Note:** The underlying marketplace and logistics architecture is designed to be regulation-ready. In compliance with Turkish Republic pharmaceutical laws, should online medication sales and delivery become legally permitted, the platform's e-commerce infrastructure and courier navigation system can be seamlessly activated and scaled.

> `React Native` · `Supabase` · `Framer Motion` · `Location Services`

---

### 🧭 PharmacyFinder *(Name change pending — it does a LOT more than finding pharmacies now 😄)*
What started as a simple pharmacy locator has evolved into a full-scale **health infrastructure navigation system** for Turkey. It maps every pharmacy, hospital, health center, dental clinic, and veterinary clinic across the country onto an interactive **3D map**. Powered by a **custom-built route calculation engine**, it delivers live turn-by-turn navigation with real-time **maneuver guidance** — a proprietary navigation program built from the ground up.

Together with **MedicationAlert**, these two projects form a unified **health-tech ecosystem** that is being prepared for public release on app stores.

> `React Native` · `3D Map Engine` · `Custom Route Calculator` · `Live Navigation` · `Maneuver Engine` · **[In Active Development]**

---

### [🖼️ RemoovBG — Intelligent Background Removal](https://www.remoovbg.com)
A production-grade SaaS tool purpose-built for the **Print-on-Demand** workflow. Sellers preparing product designs can strip backgrounds from images using a dual-engine approach: an **AI-powered model** for complex scenes and a precision **pixel-level engine** for clean cutouts. The standout feature is **Bulk Operations** — users can upload and process hundreds of images simultaneously, with all results automatically packaged into a single **ZIP download** ready for immediate use.

> `Next.js` · `AI Engine` · `Pixel Processing` · `Bulk Pipeline` · `SEO Optimized` · **[Live at remoovbg.com](https://www.remoovbg.com)**
